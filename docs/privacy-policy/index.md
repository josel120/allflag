# Privacy Policy for AllFlag

**Effective date: September 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AllFlag (Android package `com.allflag.allflag`) handles information in version 0.9.0+11.

## 1. Introduction

AllFlag is an offline application for displaying country flags. It currently includes 195 country flags. AllFlag does not require an account or login, does not contain advertising, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information We Collect

AllFlag does not collect personal information through an application backend and does not transmit user information to AllFlag servers. The application does not request dangerous Android runtime permissions and does not access location, contacts, the camera, microphone, photos, health information, financial information, or other protected personal resources.

Search text and the current normal flag selection are session state. They are not intended to be stored as persistent user data.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

AllFlag stores some preferences locally in normal application-private device storage so that the application can provide its features. These preferences may include:

- Favorite flags
- Up to five recent flag identifiers
- Quick Flag selection
- Selected appearance or theme
- Selected language

Country flag images and catalog metadata are bundled with the application and are available offline.

## 4. How We Use Information

The locally stored preferences are used only to provide and remember the corresponding AllFlag features, such as displaying favorites, recent flags, Quick Flag, and your selected appearance or language. AllFlag does not use this information for advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling.

## 5. Data Sharing

AllFlag does not sell or share personal information with third parties. AllFlag does not transmit user information to AllFlag servers.

## 6. Third-Party Services

AllFlag does not integrate analytics, tracking, or advertising services, and it does not use an application backend. The current version does not intentionally transmit user information to third-party services as part of AllFlag's functionality.

## 7. Device Backups

Depending on your device and account configuration, Android operating-system backup and restore mechanisms may include application preferences. This process is controlled by the operating system and device settings. As a result, locally stored preferences may be included in a device backup or restored to a device according to those settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

AllFlag does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because AllFlag does not require accounts and does not transmit user information to AllFlag servers, the application is designed to function without collecting personal information.

## 9. Data Security

AllFlag stores its preferences in normal application-private device storage. We do not claim that these preferences are encrypted. The security of data stored on the device and any operating-system backup is also affected by the device's security settings and the operating system.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

AllFlag retains the locally stored preferences while they remain on your device or are restored through an operating-system backup. You can clear locally stored application data through Android or device settings, or delete it by uninstalling AllFlag. Session state, including search text and the current normal flag selection, is not intended as persistent user data.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when AllFlag's information practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this page, and its effective date will be updated accordingly.
