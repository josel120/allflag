# Privacy Policy for AllFlag

**Effective date: November 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how AllFlag (Android package `com.allflag.allflag`) handles information in version 1.0.0 and later. Public address: https://josel120.github.io/politicas/allflag/privacidad/

## 1. Introduction

AllFlag is an application for displaying country flags. It includes 195 country flags and works offline. AllFlag does not require an account or login and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

AllFlag shows **no ads anywhere in the app except one optional screen**, "Support the developer", where you can choose to watch a rewarded ad. Nothing in AllFlag is locked behind ads.

## 2. Information We Collect

AllFlag has no backend and does not send information to AllFlag servers. It does not request dangerous Android runtime permissions and does not access location, contacts, the camera, microphone, photos, health or financial information.

If, and only if, you open "Support the developer", the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) are started on that screen. At that point Google may collect and process, under Google's own policies:

- Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (where available and permitted by your settings and consent)
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
- Ad interaction and performance data (ad requests, impressions, clicks, crashes of the ad SDK)
- Your consent choices, where a consent message is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland)

These data are used to request, show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, depending on your consent, personalize ads. If you never open "Support the developer", AllFlag does not start the ad SDK and does not request ads.

Search text and the current normal flag selection are session state and are not stored as persistent user data.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

AllFlag stores some preferences locally in normal application-private device storage:

- Favorite flags
- Up to five recent flag identifiers
- Quick Flag selection
- Selected theme (System, Light or Dark)
- Selected language
- The number of times you have supported the developer by watching an ad (your supporter badge)

Country flag images and catalog metadata are bundled with the application and available offline. These preferences never leave your device through AllFlag.

## 4. How We Use Information

Local preferences are used only to provide AllFlag's features. The supporter count is shown only to you, as a thank-you. Ad-related data is processed by Google, as described above, only to provide the optional rewarded ad.

## 5. Data Sharing

AllFlag does not sell personal information. When you use "Support the developer", the data listed in section 2 is shared with Google (AdMob) as the ad provider. AllFlag does not transmit user information to its own servers.

## 6. Third-Party Services and Consent

- **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)**, ad provider. Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Google User Messaging Platform**, consent management. Where the law requires it, a consent message is shown before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with **Privacy options** on the "Support the developer" screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by device).

The "Privacy policy" link in AllFlag opens this page in your browser; AllFlag itself does not load web pages.

## 7. Device Backups

Depending on your device and account configuration, Android backup and restore may include application preferences, including the supporter count. This is controlled by the operating system and your device settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

AllFlag is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional ad screen is not intended for children.

## 9. Data Security

AllFlag stores its preferences in normal application-private device storage. We do not claim these preferences are encrypted. Ad traffic handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear AllFlag's data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for ads is retained under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and Android ad settings.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when AllFlag's practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this page with a new effective date.
